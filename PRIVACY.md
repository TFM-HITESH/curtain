# Privacy policy

Curtain is built so that your data never leaves your browser. This policy applies to the Curtain browser extension.

## What Curtain stores

Curtain stores a small amount of data in your browser's local extension storage:

- Whether Guest mode is on, and when it was turned on.
- The IDs of chats and items that belong to the current Guest mode session. These are removed when you exit Guest mode.
- The IDs of chats you chose to show in Guest mode.
- Your PIN as a salted, one-way hash, never the PIN itself, plus a count of recent wrong attempts.
- Your Curtain settings.

Curtain never stores chat titles, chat content, messages, files or account details.

## What Curtain never does

- It never sends data anywhere. Curtain makes no network requests of its own.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising.
- It never loads code from the internet. Everything it runs ships inside the extension.
- It never sells, shares or transfers data, because it never has any.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** to keep the data listed above on your device.
- **Access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com:** to hide past content and add Guest mode controls on those sites. Curtain reads pages on these sites only to decide what to hide and runs nowhere else.

## Reporting a problem

When you use **Report a problem** in the Curtain popup, Curtain shows you the exact details it will include (your Curtain and browser versions, the site, and which parts of the page it could find) and opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub. Nothing is submitted until you review it and submit it yourself on GitHub, where GitHub's own privacy policy applies.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Curtain deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are made in this repository, so every revision is public in its history.
